## A portfolio with a hinge

The portfolio is built as a small device instead of a page: the menu is a
finite carousel, the top screen previews the current signal, and the bottom
screen is the place to touch into it.

- **Stack:** Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind, Three.js
- **Direction:** graphite hardware, ice-blue status lights, cobalt interaction
- **Constraint:** the Wii prototype remains available as an archived branch
